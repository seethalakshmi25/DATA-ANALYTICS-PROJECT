📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the business performance of **Bharat Herald**, a newspaper organization experiencing changes in print circulation, advertising revenue, and digital audience engagement.

Using Microsoft Power BI, I developed an interactive dashboard to examine historical business performance, identify operational challenges, evaluate digital readiness, and explore opportunities for revenue growth.

The project combines primary data analysis with secondary business analysis to develop actionable recommendations for Bharat Herald's digital transformation.

🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project were:

- Analyze print circulation trends from 2019 to 2024.
- Identify the highest-performing cities based on copies sold and net circulation.
- Examine differences between copies printed and net circulation to understand print wastage.
- Evaluate advertising revenue trends across different categories.
- Understand the relationship between advertising revenue and print circulation.
- Assess digital readiness and digital pilot engagement across cities.
- Identify cities that should be prioritized for a digital relaunch.
- Recommend strategies to improve advertiser confidence and recover revenue.

📊 Dashboard Analysis

The Power BI dashboard explores the following analytical areas:

1. Print Circulation Trends

Analyzed copies printed, copies sold, and net circulation between 2019 and 2024.

**Key analytical focus:**
- Year-over-year circulation changes.
- Changes in print demand.
- Differences between production and actual circulation.
- Long-term trends in newspaper readership.

**Business implication:** Understanding print circulation trends helps the organization make better decisions about printing volumes, distribution, and operational costs.

2. Top-Performing Cities

Evaluated city-level performance using copies sold and net circulation.

**Key analytical focus:**
- Identification of leading cities.
- Comparison of city-level circulation performance.
- Understanding which markets contribute significantly to print distribution.

**Business implication:** High-performing cities can be considered important markets for retaining advertisers and strengthening customer relationships.

3. Print Waste Analysis

Examined the gap between copies printed and net circulation across cities.

**Key analytical focus:**
- Identifying cities with larger circulation gaps.
- Comparing print wastage across locations.
- Understanding changes in distribution efficiency.

**Business implication:** Better demand forecasting and city-level print planning can help reduce unnecessary printing and distribution costs.

4. Advertising Revenue Trends by Category

Analyzed advertising revenue across different advertising categories between 2019 and 2024.

**Key analytical focus:**
- Revenue contribution by advertising category.
- Changes in category-level advertising performance.
- Identification of categories showing stronger or weaker revenue trends.

**Business implication:** Understanding category-level performance can help Bharat Herald develop more focused advertising strategies and diversify its revenue sources.

5. Digital Readiness vs. Performance

Compared digital readiness indicators, including smartphone penetration, internet access, and literacy rates, with digital pilot engagement.

**Key analytical focus:**
- Differences in digital readiness across cities.
- Digital pilot engagement levels.
- Users reached and downloads or accesses.
- Identification of markets with potential for digital growth.

**Business implication:** Digital readiness alone does not guarantee engagement. The organization must also consider content relevance, accessibility, and audience preferences.

6. Digital Relaunch City Prioritization

Evaluated cities using digital readiness, pilot engagement, and print decline to identify suitable markets for the first phase of digital transformation.

**Key analytical focus:**
- Comparing digital readiness with actual engagement.
- Considering declining print circulation.
- Identifying cities with opportunities for digital expansion.
- Prioritizing markets for a phased digital relaunch.

**Business implication:** A phased approach allows Bharat Herald to test digital strategies in promising markets before expanding to other cities.

7. Advertising Revenue vs. Circulation ROI

Examined advertising revenue in relation to net circulation to understand the revenue generated relative to print readership.

**Key analytical focus:**
- Comparing advertising revenue with net circulation.
- Identifying differences in revenue efficiency across cities.
- Understanding how advertising performance relates to print readership.

**Business implication:** Revenue per circulated copy can provide an additional perspective on the commercial performance of different markets.

💡 Key Business Insights & Recommendations

Based on the analytical framework and dashboard, the following are the major strategic insights developed for Bharat Herald.

1. Adopt a Phased Digital Transformation Strategy

Rather than moving away from print immediately, Bharat Herald should gradually introduce digital platforms while maintaining its existing print audience.

- Begin with cities demonstrating stronger digital readiness and promising engagement.
- Expand digital services based on pilot performance.
- Maintain print distribution in markets where readership remains important.
- Monitor digital adoption and audience engagement regularly.

2. Rebuild Advertiser Confidence

Advertising revenue recovery requires stronger relationships with advertisers.

- Introduce transparent advertising performance reports.
- Provide city-level audience and circulation insights.
- Develop targeted advertising packages.
- Communicate measurable campaign performance to advertisers.

3. Improve Digital Content Delivery

Digital engagement can be improved by making content easier to access and consume.

Recommended initiatives include:

- WhatsApp news bulletins.
- Mobile-optimized e-papers.
- Short-form regional news updates.
- Personalized news notifications.
- Interactive digital content.

4. Explore Alternative Revenue Models

To reduce dependence on traditional print advertising, Bharat Herald can explore additional revenue streams.

- Digital subscription packages.
- Print and digital subscription bundles.
- Loyalty and membership programs.
- Pay-per-article access.
- Premium regional news content.

5. Strengthen Regional Digital Credibility

Local trust is essential for building a sustainable digital audience.

- Collaborate with regional journalists.
- Engage local influencers.
- Promote community-focused reporting.
- Publish regional-language content.
- Encourage audience participation and feedback.

📈 Overall Project Takeaways

This project demonstrates how business data can be transformed into actionable insights.

The analysis highlights the importance of looking beyond individual revenue and circulation figures. A sustainable business strategy requires understanding operational efficiency, advertiser confidence, changing consumer behavior, and digital adoption together.

The key strategic direction for Bharat Herald is to **balance its traditional print business with a gradual, data-driven digital transformation**, while developing alternative revenue opportunities and strengthening relationships with readers and advertisers.
